# Variable Practice Questions

Practice these questions to strengthen your understanding of Python Variables.

---

# Basic Level

### 1. Store and Print Your Name

Create a variable named `name` and store your name in it. Print the variable.

#### Example

```text
Output:
Rahul
```

---

### 2. Store Age and City

Create variables for your age and city. Print them in a meaningful sentence.

#### Example

```text
Output:
I am 18 years old and I live in Varanasi.
```

---

### 3. Sum of Two Numbers

Store two numbers in variables and print their sum.

#### Example

```text
Input:
10, 20

Output:
30
```

---

### 4. Swap Two Variables

Store values in two variables and swap them.

#### Example

```text
Before:
a = 10
b = 20

After:
a = 20
b = 10
```

---

### 5. Print Data Types

Create variables of type:

- Integer
- Float
- String
- Boolean

Print their data types using `type()`.

#### Example

```text
Output:
<class 'int'>
<class 'float'>
<class 'str'>
<class 'bool'>
```

---

# Intermediate Level

### 6. Area of a Rectangle

Store the length and breadth of a rectangle in variables and calculate its area.

#### Formula

```text
Area = Length × Breadth
```

#### Example

```text
Input:
Length = 10
Breadth = 5

Output:
50
```

---

### 7. Calculate Your Age

Store your birth year in a variable and calculate your current age.

#### Example

```text
Input:
Birth Year = 2007

Output:
18
```

> Assume the current year is 2025 unless otherwise specified.

---

### 8. Area of a Circle

Store the radius of a circle in a variable and calculate its area.

#### Formula

```text
Area = π × r²
```

#### Example

```text
Input:
Radius = 7

Output:
153.94
```

---

### 9. Find the Average of Three Numbers

Store three numbers in variables and calculate their average.

#### Formula

```text
Average = (a + b + c) / 3
```

#### Example

```text
Input:
10, 20, 30

Output:
20
```

---

### 10. Calculate Total Marks

Store marks of 5 subjects in separate variables and calculate the total marks.

#### Example

```text
Input:
80, 90, 75, 88, 92

Output:
425
```

---

# Challenge Questions

### 11. Convert Temperature

Store temperature in Celsius and convert it to Fahrenheit.

#### Formula

```text
F = (C × 9/5) + 32
```

---

### 12. Calculate Simple Interest

Store:

- Principal
- Rate
- Time

and calculate Simple Interest.

#### Formula

```text
SI = (P × R × T) / 100
```

---

### 13. Calculate BMI

Store weight and height in variables and calculate BMI.

#### Formula

```text
BMI = Weight / (Height × Height)
```

---

### 14. Find the Greatest of Three Numbers

Store three numbers in variables and find the greatest one.

#### Example

```text
Input:
10, 25, 15

Output:
25
```

---

### 15. Calculate Total and Percentage

Store marks of 5 subjects and calculate:

- Total Marks
- Percentage

#### Example

```text
Input:
80, 90, 75, 88, 92

Output:
Total = 425
Percentage = 85%
```
